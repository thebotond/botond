Research interests

My research investigates how deep learning, statistical modelling, and evaluation frameworks can support the generation and validation of synthetic tabular population data.

  • Deep generative models for synthetic tabular data
  • Scenario-conditioned synthetic populations
  • Microsimulation and population projection
  • Dependency aware modelling and evaluation
  • Explainable machine learning

Current PhD research

Currently, I am in the process of developing an evaluation framework for synthetic population tabular data. The aim of the framework is to check the quality of these data in multiple dimensions and determine the usefulness of the data and the quality of the generator.

There are many directions one can go when it comes to modelling tabular data. In healthcare, it is important that an AI/ML system is explainable which creates an interesting challenge as deep generative models don't tend to be inherently explainable.

LLMs are very powerful when it comes to dealing with users. A user can be, i.e., a public health policy maker who describes a scenario and feeds it into an LLM as a prompt. The challenge is to train an (not so large) LLM to process the prompt and then have it generate a conditional vector that we can apply to projections.
