Research interests

My research investigates how deep learning, statistical modelling, and evaluation frameworks can support the generation and validation of synthetic tabular population data.

  • Synthetic tabular data generation
  • Deep generative models for public health data
  • Scenario-conditioned synthetic populations
  • Microsimulation and population projection
  • Dependency-aware modelling and evaluation
  • Explainable and statistically grounded machine learning

Current PhD research

My PhD focuses on generating close-to-reality synthetic population data using deep learning and statistical modelling, with particular attention to forward and backward projections, model evaluation, and public health relevance.
