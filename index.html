About

I am a PhD researcher interested in the development and evaluation of machine learning methods for structured, tabular, and population-level data. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, statistics, epidemiology, and public health.

My current research focuses on synthetic tabular data generation, microsimulation, scenario-conditioned population modelling, and transparent evaluation frameworks for synthetic health data.

Current focus

Synthetic data

Generating realistic and useful synthetic tabular data while preserving statistical structure and practical utility.

Public health modelling

Using data science and machine learning to support epidemiological analysis and population-level modelling.

Local AI systems

Building practical AI tools for document search, retrieval-augmented generation, research support, and reproducible workflows.
