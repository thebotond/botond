About

I am a PhD researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics, epidemiology, and public health. My current research focuses on synthetic tabular data, population modelling, deep generative models, and microsimulation.

Research

My research investigates how machine learning and statistical modelling can be used to generate realistic, useful, and interpretable synthetic population data. I am particularly interested in tabular data generation, dependency-aware modelling, scenario conditioning, evaluation frameworks, and public health applications.

Research interests

  • Deep generative models for population health data
  • Microsimulation and population projection
  • Evaluation frameworks for synthetic data
  • Explainable and statistically grounded machine learning
