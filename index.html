About

I am a PhD researcher interested in the development and evaluation of machine learning methods for structured, tabular data, specifically public health data. My work is a mix of a few things: artificial intelligence, statistics, epidemiology, and public health.

Current Work

My current research focuses on synthetic tabular data generation, microsimulation, scenario-conditioned population modelling and transparent evaluation frameworks for synthetic health data. You can find more on this under "Research".

Besides my PhD research, I have been very heavily involved in teaching at the School of Computer Science and Informatics at the University of Liverpool over the past few years. I tend to TA for AI and data-related modules (for more information, please see the "Teaching" page).

I have been involved in various commercial projects. I am currently a director at Pannon Technologies. It is a small family business which started as an engineering shop and we recently decided to branch out a little bit. You can find more on this under "Services".

I enjoy working on different things - feel free to visit "Personal Projects" to see what I am up to and if it is of any use to you. I will also be posting about things I learn - you will find these on the "Resources" page - if you are student, you might want to have a look.
